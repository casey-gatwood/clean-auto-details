Paint Correction

Swirls, scratches & dull paint — corrected, not covered up

Our Full Detail adds a 2-step paint correction and buffing on top of the Base Detail, finished with a 1-year paint sealant. For paint that needs more, the Full Buffing Service goes further: wet sanding plus a 3-step correction, topped with a 2-year ceramic coating.

Not sure which level your paint needs? Book a Base or Full Detail and we’ll look the paint over with you at drop-off and confirm the right service and the exact price before we start.

Interior Detailing

An interior that feels new again

The Base Detail is a true full interior cleaning — headliner, carpet, dash, doors, seats, and the ventilation system — with every piece of glass finished crystal clear. All plastic and leather is treated with a non-greasy product, so it looks rich without feeling slick.

Just need a refresh? The Quick Detail covers a full exterior wash, vacuum, interior wipe-down, door jambs, and all glass.

Good to Know

Questions, answered

Why is every price a range?

A clean sedan and a work truck that hasn’t been washed in a year aren’t the same job. Each package is priced by your vehicle’s size and condition, and we confirm your exact price with you before any work starts — the range on the package is the range you’ll land in.

How do I pay?

We accept Square (card), cash, Cash App, PayPal, and Venmo. Our Cash App is $CaseyGatwood, our PayPal is 1ridetohigh@gmail.com, and you can pay us on Venmo. Pick your preferred method when you book — nothing is charged on this page. When we confirm your appointment by phone or text, we’ll arrange payment with that method, with the balance due when your vehicle is done.

What does the ceramic coating warranty cover?

Our 2-year coating comes with a 1-year warranty, the 5-year coating with a 3-year warranty, and the 7-year coating with a 5-year warranty. We’ll go over the warranty details for your tier when you book.

Do you do work besides detailing?

Yes — custom bodywork and paint jobs, sound damping installs, headlight restoration, and more. Those are quoted individually, so call (918) 329-9259 or add them to your booking request and tell us what you need.

Where are you, and when are you open?

414 3rd Ave in Warner, Oklahoma. We’re open Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM.
